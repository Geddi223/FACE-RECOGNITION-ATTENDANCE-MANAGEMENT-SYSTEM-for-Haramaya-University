Check-In Procedures

Valid Check-In Times:

You can mark your arrival between 7:30 AM and 9:30 AM. Late arrivals after 9:30 AM require supervisor approval.

Location Verification:

You must be within designated university locations. The system uses GPS to verify your location.

Facial Recognition:

Position your face in the camera view and follow the on-screen instructions for liveness detection.

System Requirements:

Ensure your device has a working camera and location services enabled for proper authentication.
