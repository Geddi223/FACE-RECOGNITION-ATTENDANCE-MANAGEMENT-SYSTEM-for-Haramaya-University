About the System

How It Works

The Face Recognition Employee Attendance Management System (FREAMS) uses state-of-the-art artificial intelligence to verify employee identities through facial features. The system captures facial data, compares it with registered profiles, and records attendance with precise timestamps.

Key components include:

  • High-resolution cameras for accurate facial capture
  • Machine learning algorithms for facial feature analysis
  • Secure database for employee profiles
  • Real-time reporting interface
  • Integration with HR management systems

Security & Privacy

We prioritize the security and privacy of all users:

  • Facial data is encrypted and stored securely
  • No personal biometric data is shared with third parties
  • System complies with Ethiopian data protection regulations
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
  • Regular security audits and updates
Your facial data is used solely for attendance purposes and is protected under Haramaya University's data privacy policy.

About Haramaya University

Haramaya University is one of Ethiopia's oldest and most prestigious higher education institutions, established in 1954. Located in the eastern part of Ethiopia, the university has grown to become a center of excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement.

The university offers undergraduate and graduate programs across various disciplines including agriculture, health sciences, natural sciences, social sciences, and humanities. With a commitment to quality education and research, Haramaya University continues to contribute significantly to Ethiopia's development.
