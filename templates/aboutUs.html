Our University at a Glance

Mission

The mission of Haramaya University is to train competent graduates in diverse fields of study, undertake knowledge-generating, problem-solving and cutting-edge research, and provide demand-driven and transformative community engagement activities that contribute to sustainable local, national, regional and global development.

Vision

Haramaya University strives to be one of the top ten universities in Eastern Africa with an international reputation by 2030. We aim to be a center of excellence in teaching, research, and community service.

Goal

To evolve as an autonomous and independent graduate and research university with competitive national and international quality and relevance standards in its academic, research, and community engagement endeavors.

Core Values

Excellence, innovation, social responsibility, integrity, embracing diversity and inclusiveness, democratic culture, good governance, teamwork and collaboration, lifelong learning, and respect for nature and the environment.

Our History

A legacy of academic excellence and community service

1954

Establishment

Founded as the Imperial Ethiopian College of Agricultural and Mechanical Arts (IECAMA) by Emperor Haile Selassie I with assistance from Oklahoma State University.

1967

Name Change

Renamed to Alemaya College of Agriculture, becoming a faculty of Haile Selassie I University (now Addis Ababa University).

1985

Autonomous Status

Gained autonomous status as Alemaya University of Agriculture under the Provisional Military Government of Socialist Ethiopia.

2006

Current Name

Renamed to Haramaya University, expanding its scope beyond agriculture to become a comprehensive university.

Present

Modern University

Now one of Ethiopia's leading universities with multiple campuses, offering diverse programs in agriculture, health sciences, natural sciences, social sciences, and humanities.

Our Campuses

Multiple locations serving diverse academic needs

Main Campus

Located in Haramaya, 15km from Harar and 40km from Dire Dawa, this is the historic heart of the university with beautiful landscapes and modern facilities.

Dire Dawa Campus

The university's urban campus offering programs in business, social sciences, and technology in Ethiopia's second largest city.

Health Sciences Campus

Dedicated to medical education and research, housing the College of Health and Medical Sciences with modern teaching hospitals.
