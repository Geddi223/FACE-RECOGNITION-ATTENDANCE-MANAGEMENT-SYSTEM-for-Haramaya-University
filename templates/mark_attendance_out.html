Check-Out Procedures

Valid Check-Out Times:

You can mark your departure between 4:30 PM and 6:30 PM. The system will automatically calculate your working hours.

Location Verification:

You must be within designated university locations. The system uses GPS to verify your location.

Facial Recognition:

Position your face in the camera view and follow the on-screen instructions for liveness detection.

Early Departures:

If you need to leave before 4:30 PM, please obtain approval from your supervisor and HR department.
