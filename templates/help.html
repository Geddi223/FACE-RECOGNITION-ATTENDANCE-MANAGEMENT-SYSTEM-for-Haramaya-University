Getting Started

How to log in to the system

To log in to the attendance system:

  1. Navigate to the login page at attendance.haramaya.edu.et/login
  2. Enter your university-issued username
  3. Enter your password (initially provided by HR)
  4. Click "Login" or press Enter
  5. For first-time login, you'll be prompted to change your password

Remember to log out after each session, especially on shared computers.

How to register a new employee (Admin only) New

Administrators can register new employees:

  1. Log in with admin credentials
  2. Navigate to "Employee Management"
  3. Click "Add New Employee"
  4. Fill in all required personal information
  5. Set initial department and position
  6. Capture facial data using the built-in camera
  7. Generate and provide temporary login credentials
Facial data capture requires good lighting and a neutral expression. Remove glasses/hats if possible.
