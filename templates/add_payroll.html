Payroll System Help
How to use this system

This payroll system allows you to calculate and process employee payments. Fill in all required fields and the system will automatically calculate the net pay.

Calculation Details
  • Base Salary: The employee's fixed monthly salary
  • Allowances: Additional regular payments (housing, transport, etc.)
  • Bonuses: One-time additional payments
  • Deductions: Amounts to be subtracted (loans, insurance, etc.)
  • Performance Rating: Affects a percentage bonus/penalty on base salary
  • Absences: Deducted at a daily rate (salary/22 working days)
