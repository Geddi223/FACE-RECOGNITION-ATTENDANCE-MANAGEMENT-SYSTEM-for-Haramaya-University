Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for student housing?

Student housing applications are available through the online student portal after admission. Priority is given to first-year students and those coming from distant regions. The housing office can provide more information about availability and application deadlines.

What health services are available on campus?

The university health center provides primary care services, emergency treatment, counseling services, and health education programs. All registered students have access to basic health services, with additional insurance options available for more comprehensive coverage.

How can I access library resources remotely?

Our digital library resources are available 24/7 through the university library website using your student credentials. This includes e-books, academic journals, databases, and research tools. Library staff also offer virtual research assistance during working hours.

What career support is available for alumni?

Alumni have lifelong access to career counseling services, job postings, and networking events. The career center offers resume reviews, interview preparation, and maintains relationships with employers to facilitate job opportunities for our graduates.

How do I apply for research funding?

The Office of Research and Community Engagement manages internal research grants and can assist with external funding applications. Funding opportunities are announced regularly, and faculty can schedule consultations with our grant specialists for proposal development support.
